## Follow the link in both directions

Traceability lets a reader start from a requirement and see what evidence supports it, or start from an artifact and see why it matters. A stable identifier is better for this purpose than a title that may change. Links should carry enough scope to distinguish versions and subjects.

A link is a relationship, not a verdict. It may show that an artifact is proposed in support of a requirement even though the review is still open. If one artifact serves multiple requirements, each relationship should express what the artifact contributes.

## Fictional example

A tray witness image supports the Fern Pathfinder visibility requirement. The image also lets the reviewer jump back to the build configuration used for that observation.
