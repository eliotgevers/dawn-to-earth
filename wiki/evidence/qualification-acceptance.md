## Two different questions

| Activity | What the evidence concerns | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualification | A defined design and configuration | Can the design meet its requirements in its intended environment? |
| Acceptance | An individual manufactured article | Does this article conform to the applicable qualified design? |

A design’s qualification evidence has a defined scope. Acceptance concerns a particular article and its documented state. A qualified design can have one article accepted and another article still under review. A later reviewer should be able to see which serial, configuration, and evidence support each decision.

## Why the two records stay separate

The NASA handbook describes qualification as establishing that a flight design meets requirements in its anticipated environment. Acceptance applies to each manufactured unit and focuses on conformance and workmanship. When a design revision changes, the applicability of earlier design evidence is a separate question from the acceptance status of any unit.

Dawn’s 2024 lunar-relay announcement described evaluating electronics for the lunar environment. It is a public example of discussing qualification in terms of a design and an environment. The table above uses NASA’s terminology, and the example below is independent of the reported mission.
