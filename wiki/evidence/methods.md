## Methods answer different questions

| Method | General evidence it contributes |
| --- | --- |
| Analysis | Reasoned assessment from a model or data |
| Inspection | Observed attributes of an article or record |
| Demonstration | Observable behavior in a stated context |
| Test | Measurements or outcomes under specified conditions |

The method name does not establish that the evidence is adequate. A reviewer still needs to know the applicable requirement and what an observation represents. Several evidence types can complement one another without being interchangeable. For example, a clear image of what was assembled answers a different question than an analysis of the expected behavior.

## Fictional example

A fictional cradle image establishes whether the witness is visible in the recorded view. The image alone cannot establish the behavior of the assembly in another context.
