## From source to review

Provenance describes where an artifact came from and how it was carried into a record. It includes the source or author when known, the capture identity, and the version reviewed. A derived chart or summary should point to the original material from which it was made.

Changes in labeling, format, or processing can alter how an observation is interpreted. For example, an export can omit metadata present in the original. Record the relationship instead of renaming an export as though it were the source. A provenance gap is separate from the technical outcome of the review.
