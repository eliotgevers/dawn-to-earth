## Requirements and use

Verification asks whether the product meets specified requirements. Validation asks whether the resulting product serves the intended use under the conditions that matter to its users. A design can satisfy a stated requirement while still falling short of what stakeholders intended.

A record can cite the same observation in both discussions, but the conclusions remain tied to their different questions. State the requirement for a verification claim and the intended use for a validation claim. The same word “passed” is ambiguous without that context.

## Fictional example

A readable offline archive index satisfies the fictional identifier requirement. A separate exercise shows whether the receiving team can actually locate its rehearsal dataset.
