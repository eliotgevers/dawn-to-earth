## Mapping one requirement at a time

A verification matrix organizes the association between requirements and their planned or actual verification evidence. It can also show the method, the relevant level of assembly, and the outcome. A single row may need to link multiple artifacts when no one artifact addresses the whole requirement.

| Matrix entry | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Requirement ID | Stable identity of the expectation |
| Method | How the evidence addresses the question in broad terms |
| Applicable evidence | The record that can be reviewed |
| Disposition | The conclusion, when one exists |

A filled evidence column and a settled disposition are worth counting separately.

## Fictional example

A fictional matrix includes an assembled-view requirement, an image link, and an open review. The requirement has evidence coverage without a final disposition.
