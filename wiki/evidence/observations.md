## Several layers of a review record

An observation records what was seen, measured, or found in the available material. A finding relates that observation to an expectation or question. A decision says how the team treats the result within a defined scope. Keeping the layers distinct helps later reviewers judge which part changed.

An absent attachment is an observation about the evidence package. It can justify an open review. It cannot establish either a pass or a failure of the underlying article. Likewise, a note acknowledging receipt does not necessarily replace an earlier technical decision.

## Fictional example

A reviewer notes that the adjustable locator photo crops out the reference face. The finding concerns photo coverage and the decision requests a follow-up image.
