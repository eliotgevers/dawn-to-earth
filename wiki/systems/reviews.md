## The record of a decision

A technical review assesses a defined question about a design, article, or body of evidence. A useful record preserves the question and its scope, the material considered, the disposition, and the rationale. Open work belongs alongside the decision so readers can distinguish an approved item from unresolved follow-up.

An acknowledgment can record that material has been seen without changing a previous technical disposition. Likewise, a decision about a group need not settle an issue tied to one serial. If reviewers disagree, capture what each decision covered before assuming that the newer note supersedes an earlier one.

## Fictional example

A Rimu Surveyor reviewer acknowledges receipt of a photo and separately requests the missing assembled view needed to close the open question.
