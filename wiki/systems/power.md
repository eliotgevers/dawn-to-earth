## Three distinct roles

A spacecraft power system typically generates, stores, and distributes electrical energy. The availability of generation can vary over the orbit, for example when the spacecraft is in shadow. Storage and distribution serve different purposes from generation.

A description of instantaneous power says how quickly energy is used or supplied. A description of energy covers an interval. A large brief draw and a smaller sustained draw could have the same energy total while producing different demands on the spacecraft. The boundary between an instrument and its power provider belongs in the interface description. A plain-language review can identify which side supplies the service without recording any design values.
