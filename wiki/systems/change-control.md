## Proposal, decision, implementation

A change proposal states what might be different and why. Approval records a decision to change the controlled definition under the stated conditions. Implementation records that the particular change was applied to an article or configuration. These are distinct claims and can occur at different times.

A change record is most helpful when it describes the affected requirements, interfaces, revisions, or serials. A reviewer can use those links to determine whether old evidence still applies. A later approved change does not make an earlier observation untrue. It can change which question that observation answers for later builds.

## Fictional example

Fern Pathfinder approves an alternate tag position. A traveler later confirms whether that change applied to an individual demonstrator.
