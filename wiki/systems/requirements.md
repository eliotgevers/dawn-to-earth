## What must be true?

A requirement describes a needed property or behavior of the system. It should be possible to identify where it came from and what evidence could show whether it was met. Unique identifiers allow a requirement to remain traceable even when its wording changes.

A requirement and a proposed solution are different records. One can express the outcome needed at an interface without choosing the precise design. When an agreed requirement changes, its context, downstream design references, and already collected evidence may all need review. Traceability helps a team find those consequences without relying on a word search alone.

## Fictional example

A Tern Payload Cradle requirement describes a witness remaining visible in a handoff view. The location of the window belongs in the configuration record.
