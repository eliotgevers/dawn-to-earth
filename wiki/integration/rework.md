## A linked before-and-after record

A rework record describes work on an existing article after an issue or change. It should be traceable to that decision, to the article, and to the updated build state. A correction to a document can look similar in a list, but it changes the record rather than the article.

A follow-up observation may answer a question left open by an earlier record. Keeping both records allows a reviewer to see what changed. Where later evidence supersedes an earlier observation for a decision, the relationship should be explicit and scoped to the applicable article and revision.
