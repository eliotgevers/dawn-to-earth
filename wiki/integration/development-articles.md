## What the evidence applies to

A development article exists to explore or demonstrate some aspect of a system. A fixture supports the work by holding, simulating, or representing part of the setting. Depending on the question, a fixture can be the object under development itself or merely an aid for evaluating another article.

An observation from a development article is valuable when its scope is clear. Identify which properties it represents and which remain unestablished. In a record browser, fixture work can live under the development system it supports. A fixture result should not be confused with acceptance evidence for an unrelated unit.

## A public example of what a model represents

Dawn’s product overview describes an engineering model, also called a flatsat on the page, that represents the power, data, and software interfaces for hardware-in-the-loop evaluation. This describes what the model represents. In a record system, the useful distinction is between evidence tied to that model and evidence tied to a serialized flight article. Both may inform an interface discussion, with different claims about applicability.

## Fictional example

The Kea Integration Carrier has its own development build groups. The bench articles under those groups are identifiable without treating them as spacecraft.
