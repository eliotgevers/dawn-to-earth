## Identity and applicability

A serial identifies an individual article. A revision identifies a version of a design or document. A build group is a convenient way of discussing related articles, and a group label may cover several serials. These labels are complementary: the same revision can be applied to several articles, each with a distinct assembly record.

A group view should name which serials it covers. A common definition can be displayed once, but a result observed on one serial cannot be assigned to its neighbors by default. Missing information, conflicting outcomes, and genuinely shared design records should remain distinguishable.

## Fictional example

Kea LK-021 and LK-022 share a Rev B build group. One recorded observation belongs to LK-021 until an applicable record establishes coverage elsewhere.
