## Separate observation and disposition

An issue records a discrepancy, uncertainty, or concern that needs attention. A nonconformance is more specific: it records a departure from an applicable requirement or definition. A missing photograph may call for follow-up even when it does not establish a departure of the build itself.

Useful records point to the affected article, the applicable expectation, and the evidence behind the finding. A later disposition records what the team decided and why. The disposition should not alter the original observation. Keep the difference clear between confirming a physical discrepancy, correcting a record, and collecting previously absent evidence.

## Fictional example

The Rimu image is missing from the handoff packet. The initial finding documents missing evidence rather than asserting that the assembly failed.
