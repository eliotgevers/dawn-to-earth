## A record attached to an article

A traveler is a common name for a work record associated with a particular article. It tells a later reader which work or observations were recorded against that article. Its relationship to the design baseline matters because it is evidence about a build, not a replacement for the approved definition.

A traveler can point to attachments, issues, and subsequent decisions. A photo named “final” is hard to interpret without its association with a serial and event. If a note was corrected, it should remain possible to tell which record was corrected and what prompted the correction.

## Fictional example

A Rimu traveler gains a link to the assembled-carrier image. The earlier note remains part of that carrier’s history.
