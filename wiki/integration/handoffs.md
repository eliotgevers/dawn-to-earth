## What the receiving team needs

A handoff connects an article or deliverable to the records needed to understand it. That usually means identifying the applicable build and revision, recording which decisions are settled, and making open questions and missing attachments visible.

A handoff can be ready for review while an underlying technical decision is still open. Distinguish those states. If work is blocked, the record should explain what decision or evidence is missing and who can act. A receiver should be able to follow the record links without needing to reconstruct the history from a series of unrelated notes.

## Fictional example

A Kōtare Mapper handoff identifies the rehearsal dataset, its setup record, and the index that connects them.
