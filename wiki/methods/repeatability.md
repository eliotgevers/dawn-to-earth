## What stayed the same?

Repeatability concerns agreement between repeated measurements made under stated similar conditions. Reproducibility concerns agreement when relevant conditions differ. The description matters because close results obtained by the same setup do not establish that different setups give the same result.

Precision and correctness are also different. A series can agree closely and still share a bias. Record what was held constant and what changed where those facts are known. If repeated observations come from different serials or revisions, their variation may reflect the articles rather than measurement variability alone.

## Fictional example

Two readers repeatedly measure a fictional drawing on the same scale. Comparing results from another scale addresses a different question about consistency under changed conditions.
