## State what the uncertainty means

A measurement result has limited precision, and uncertainty describes the range of values reasonably attributable to the measured quantity by the stated method. A standard uncertainty is expressed with the same unit as the quantity. An expanded uncertainty is reported with a stated coverage factor when applicable.

For a nonzero result, one way to report the relative standard uncertainty is

$$
u_{\mathrm{rel}} = \frac{u(x)}{|x|}.
$$

Here $x$ is the measured value and $u(x)$ is its standard uncertainty in the same unit. Their ratio is dimensionless. The meaning relies on the uncertainty estimate being stated and comparable. A ratio alone does not describe how the measurement was made or whether it supports a requirement.

## Fictional example

A classroom ruler example reports 200 mm with a standard uncertainty of 1 mm. The relative standard uncertainty is 0.005, or 0.5%.
