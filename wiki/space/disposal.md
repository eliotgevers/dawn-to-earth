## The shared orbital environment

A satellite can stop serving its mission and still remain in orbit. In a crowded environment, inactive spacecraft and fragments can create hazards for other users of space. Disposal is a way of accounting for what remains after the mission, and the relevant concepts differ with the orbital region.

ESA environment reporting separates what is already observed from projections. As a reader, note the period covered and the population being counted. Numbers of functioning spacecraft, catalogued debris objects, and estimated smaller particles describe different things. A mission concept may record a planned end state without implying that any particular spacecraft has reached it.
