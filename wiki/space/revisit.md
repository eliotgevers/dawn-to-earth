## Opportunity versus usable data

Revisit describes the time between observation opportunities for a place or region under stated assumptions. Orbital period describes one revolution. Between revolutions Earth turns, and the instrument only sees part of the surface, so a full turn does not imply the same site is visible again.

Coverage and revisit answer different questions. Coverage says which area a dataset or mission observes. Revisit says how often observation is possible. Usable data may arrive less frequently because the viewing angle or environmental conditions affect the instrument. A coverage claim should name the region, time window, and what counts as an observation.

## Fictional example

A Kōtare Mapper report separates three potential views of a district from the two views with usable imagery.
